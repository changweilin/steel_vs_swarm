# public/js — Client Principles & Boundaries

> Scope: principles and boundaries only. No methods, no workflows, no commands.
> Global principles (authority, single source, determinism) live in root `AGENTS.md`.
> Full definitions live in `.claude/rules/` (`seams-*` by topic).
> Nothing in this file restricts skill selection or skill usage.

## Behavioral Boundaries

- **Format, content, and numbers stay separated**: file-format definitions, content entries, and numeric values live apart; classification is derived, never hand-listed.
- **Generated outputs are read-only**: learned policies and baked venue outputs MUST NOT be hand-edited.
- **Client-only stays client-only**: narrative and traversal content MUST NOT be imported by the server.
- **Presentation never simulates**: overlays, icons, and text layers MUST NOT affect the simulation; help and guidance strings have one source and MUST NOT be reimplemented by callers.
- **Queries stay separated**: unit hit-queries and terrain queries use different paths; world geometry MUST NOT be fed into unit hit-tests.
- **RNG hygiene**: world content consumes none of the shared deterministic sequence; visual variety comes from coordinate-derived seeds.
- **Cheap teardown, clean assets**: outline rendering stays single-pass with resources released on teardown; audio samples stay license-clean.
